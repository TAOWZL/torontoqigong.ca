Online qigong and neigong class

The Saturday class streams live from Toronto, coached in real time, Saturdays 10 to 11 am Eastern.

The Saturday neigong class streams live from our Toronto school every week. Online students join the same class as the people in the room, at the same time.

Why the class is live

Neigong is coached. Joining live lets the teacher see how you are moving, ask what you are noticing, and adjust one thing at a time, which a recorded video cannot do.

Setting up at home

Clear enough space to stand with your arms out and take a step in any direction. Wear clothes you can move in.

Private lessons online
