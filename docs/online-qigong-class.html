Why the class is live

Neigong is coached. Joining live lets the teacher see how you are moving, ask what you are noticing, and adjust one thing at a time, which a recorded video cannot do.

Setting up at home

Clear enough space to stand with your arms out and take a step in any direction. Wear clothes you can move in.
