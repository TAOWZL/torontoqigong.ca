Toronto Qigong / Saturday neigong class

Saturday neigong class

Saturdays from 10 to 11 am at 255 Broadview Ave and live online. Three to five qigong movements per class, coached from the inside.

The Saturday class is technically a neigong class. We use qigong forms as the vehicle and spend the hour on what happens inside them. It runs in person at our Broadview Avenue school and live online at the same time, with the same coaching for both rooms.

How the hour runs

Learn the movements. Three to five movements per class. You learn the shape first, slowly.

Coach the energetics. Most of the hour goes here. For each movement we work on where attention goes, how the breath changes, and what the state feels like in the body, then map that state across the senses so you can find it again.

Anchor and test. You run the movement as a trigger and check whether the state returns, adjusting one thing at a time.

Who the class suits

The pace is slow and coaching is given at each person's level. People who already practise a qigong form come for the energetics and the five-senses method.

If you are looking for a long flowing set done mainly as exercise, a tai chi class may suit you better. This class spends its time on what each movement does to your state.

Questions people ask

What should I wear?

Clothes you can move and stand in for an hour.

Can I drop in after the trial?

Ongoing training is the monthly subscription at $150 plus HST. The trial is the single-class option.

What if I miss a Saturday?

Come the next one. The subscription is monthly and missed classes do not carry into the next month.

How fast are the movements?

Slow, and done standing. Most of the class is attention and coaching.

Can I come in person some weeks and online others?

Yes. It is the same class at the same time, so you choose each week.
