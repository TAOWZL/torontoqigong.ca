Why train privately

A private lesson puts all of the coaching on your own state and movements. People book them when they have a specific aim, such as training a steadier state for stressful situations, a way to settle after work, or getting the Saturday movements right before adding more.

How a private lesson works

Name the state. We start with what you want to be able to call up, and when you need it.

Find it in a movement. We pick one or two qigong movements and work with them until you can find the state in your body.

Map it. We go through sight, sound, feeling, smell and taste while you are in the state, so you have a way to find it again.

Anchor it. The movement is trained as the trigger, and we test it, step out of the state, and test again.

Practise it. You leave with a short practice to repeat between lessons.
