Qigong forms we practise

Eight Pieces of Brocade, Fragrant Qigong, the Sinew Changing Classic and the Five Yin Organ Exercise, coached live in Toronto and online.

Qigong forms are the vehicle for the neigong training. These are four of the classical sets practised at the school, at 255 Broadview Ave and in the live online class.

  1. 八段錦Eight Pieces of Brocade Bāduànjǐn
    A standing set of eight movements, and one of the most widely practised qigong sets.
  2. 香功Fragrant Qigong Xiānggōng
    Done standing, with slow movements of the hands and arms.
  3. 易筋經Sinew Changing Classic Yìjīnjīng
    A set that takes its name from an old text of the same title.
  4. 五臟Five Yin Organ Exercise Wǔ zàng
    Organised around the five yin organs of Chinese medicine: Liver, Heart, Spleen, Lung and Kidney.

How the forms are used in class

A Saturday class takes three to five movements from a form and coaches the energetics of each one. The form gives you the shape. The coaching covers where attention sits, what changes in the breath, and how to map the state that a movement produces across the five senses so you can return to it. How we teach neigong.

Why the forms are taught live

The shape of a movement can be copied from anywhere. The energetics come from coaching: what the attention does, what the breath does, and which state the movement anchors. Every form here is taught in class, in person or live online, with the teacher watching and adjusting.

Classes are a practice of attention and state and are not medical advice. For any health concern, talk with your doctor.

Questions people ask

Which form will I learn first?

Ask when you book. Each class works on three to five movements, and the coaching is given at each person's level.

Can I learn the forms online?

Yes. The Saturday class runs live online at the same time as the room, and private lessons are available online too.

Where are classes held?

At 255 Broadview Ave, ground floor, in Riverdale, Toronto, and live online.
