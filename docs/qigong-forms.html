Qigong forms we practise

Eight Pieces of Brocade, Fragrant Qigong, the Sinew Changing Classic and the Five Yin Organ Exercise, with follow-along practice videos.

Qigong forms are the vehicle for the neigong training. These are the sets shown in the school's practice videos on Instagram. Each link opens a short follow-along clip filmed at Wuji Xuan in Riverdale.

  1. 八段錦Eight Pieces of Brocade Bāduànjǐn
    A standing set of eight movements, and one of the most widely practised qigong sets. Watch the practice video
  2. 香功Fragrant Qigong Xiānggōng
    Done standing, with slow movements of the hands and arms. Watch the practice video
  3. 易筋經Sinew Changing Classic Yìjīnjīng
    A set that takes its name from an old text of the same title. Watch the practice video
  4. 五臟Five Yin Organ Exercise Wǔ zàng
    Organised around the five yin organs of Chinese medicine: Liver, Heart, Spleen, Lung and Kidney. Watch the practice video

How the forms are used in class

A Saturday class takes three to five movements from a form and coaches the energetics of each one. The form gives you the shape. The coaching covers where attention sits, what changes in the breath, and how to map the state that a movement produces across the five senses so you can return to it. How we teach neigong.

Practising at home

The videos are short follow-along clips for practice between classes. They are practice videos, not medical advice. For any health concern, talk with your doctor.

Questions people ask

Which form will I learn first?

Ask when you book. Each class works on three to five movements, and the coaching is given at each person's level.

Can I follow the videos without coming to class?

Yes. They are free on Instagram at @wuji_xuan. The class adds live coaching on the energetics.
